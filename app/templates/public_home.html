About Agri-Direct

What is Agri-Direct?

Agri-Direct is designed to support agricultural users through digital tools for agricultural trading, information sharing, communication, inventory/product management, and market intelligence. It brings together the people, products, and insights needed to make agricultural participation more efficient and informed.

  • Discover and manage agricultural products with confidence.
  • Learn from agricultural knowledge, updates, and shared expertise.
  • Connect with buyers, sellers, and community members.
  • Use data-driven market intelligence to support smarter decisions.
