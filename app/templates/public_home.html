About Agri-Direct

A simpler way to buy and sell farm produce

Agri-Direct brings farmers and buyers together in one easy-to-use place. Farmers can post what they have and the price they want. Buyers can browse available produce and connect with sellers directly.

  • See produce and prices posted by local sellers.
  • Share your harvest with people looking to buy.
  • Ask questions and connect directly.
  • Get started with a few simple steps.
