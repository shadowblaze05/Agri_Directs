About Agri-Direct

Agri-Direct is a real-time agricultural supply chain and market optimization platform designed to strengthen coordination between farmers, buyers, and administrators.

The system combines inventory management, live dashboard analytics, and algorithm-driven decision support to improve transparency, reduce market inefficiencies, and support data-informed agricultural decisions.

Core capabilities
  • Secure multi-user authentication and role-based access control
  • Inventory and harvest management for farmers and administrators
  • Real-time market price monitoring and supply-risk detection
  • Crop recommendation and demand forecasting based on historical trends
  • Direct messaging and an in-app AgriBot assistant
